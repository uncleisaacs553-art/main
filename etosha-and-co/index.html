01Our Story

A Taste Born of Wild Land

Etosha & Co. was born from a single belief — that the untamed beauty of Namibia deserves a seat at the world's finest tables.

Founded in 2013 by Chef Amara Nghipandulwa, a Windhoek native trained at Le Cordon Bleu in Paris, our kitchen bridges two worlds: the ancient flavours of the Namib — game from the plains, seafood from the icy Benguela Current, botanicals from the Kalahari — carried by contemporary European technique.

Every plate tells a story of place. Every ingredient is Namibian. Every evening is composed to be unforgettable.

i.
100% Namibian Sourcing

Every ingredient from Namibian farms, waters and wilderness — nothing imported, nothing anonymous.

ii.
Cordon Bleu Precision

A Paris-trained brigade with twenty years of fine-dining mastery behind every course.

iii.
A Curated Cellar

Over 200 South African and international labels, kept at cellar temperature beneath the dining room.
