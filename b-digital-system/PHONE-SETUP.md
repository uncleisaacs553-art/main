# B Digital on your phone 📱

A 5-minute, once-off setup so your whole business runs from one icon on your home
screen — plus alerts so new leads come to you instead of you checking for them.

There are three quick parts. Do them in order.

---

## 1. Put the hub on your home screen

The **hub** is one page with big buttons: Leads, Content, WhatsApp, your lead page,
and quote emails. Add it once and it behaves like an app.

1. On your phone, open: **https://uncleisaacs553-art.github.io/main/hub/**
2. Add it to your home screen:
   - **iPhone (Safari):** tap **Share** (the box with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
   - **Android (Chrome):** tap **⋮** (top-right) → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**.
3. You'll now see a **B Digital** icon on your phone. Tap it any time.

---

## 2. Connect your Notion links (one time)

On the hub, the **Leads** and **Content** buttons start as *"Tap to set up"*. You paste
your two Notion links once, and they're saved **only on your phone** (never put online).

1. Open the **Notion app** and go to your **Leads** database.
2. Tap **•••** (top-right) → **Copy link**.
3. Back on the hub, tap **Leads** (it jumps to the setup box) and **paste** the link.
4. Do the same for your **Content calendar** database.
5. Tap **Save to this phone**. Done — both buttons now open straight into Notion.

> If you ever clear your browser data or use a new phone, just repeat this step.

---

## 3. Get the Notion app set up nicely

1. Install **Notion** (App Store / Google Play) and **log in with the same account** you
   used when we set up B Digital.
2. Open the **Leads** database → tap the **⭐ / Favorite** (or "Add to sidebar") so it
   always sits at the top.
3. Show the best leads first: tap the view name → **Sort** → **AI Score** →
   **Descending**. Now your highest-scoring prospects are always on top.

---

## 4. Alerts — so leads come to you

You're covered by two layers:

- **Daily email alert (automatic, reliable).** Every day the Lead Finder finds new
  prospects, you get **one short email** — the top leads with their scores and a tap-through
  link into Notion. Nothing to switch on; just allow notifications for your email app so it
  buzzes your phone.
  - It goes to your contact email by default. To send it somewhere else, change
    `recipient` under `notifications:` in `config.yaml`.
  - To turn it off, set `new_lead_digest: false` in the same place.
- **Notion app notifications (optional extra).** In Notion → **Settings → Notifications**,
  turn on **mobile push**. Heads-up: Notion's push for rows added *automatically* (by the
  system) is limited — that's exactly why the **daily email above is your main alert**.
