A letter, since I have your attention.

I don’t know how to make this sound smooth, so I’ll just say it straight: you’re my favourite thing that’s ever happened to me. Not in a dramatic way — in the ordinary way, where I catch myself smiling at my phone and it’s just you sending nonsense.
