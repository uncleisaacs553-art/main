About the school

A focused place to do real academic work.

Bells Private School delivers quality education through focused tutorial programmes, qualified staff, and learner-centred support systems that empower students to reach their full academic potential. We are registered with Namibia's Ministry of Education under the Directorate of National Examinations and Assessment (DNEA), and operate as an official NSSCO examination centre on Dodge Street, Khomasdal.

Admissions

How to enrol

Joining is straightforward. Most learners can start within a week of their first enquiry.

  1. 1

    Enquire

    Call, message, or send the form below. Tell us the learner's grade and the subjects they need.

  2. 2

    Meet & assess

    We meet the learner, talk through goals, and run a short baseline assessment to place them correctly.

  3. 3

    Enrol & register

    Complete enrolment and, where needed, we register you for NSSCO or NAMCOL examinations as a centre.

  4. 4

    Begin classes

    Classes start, mentorship begins, and progress is tracked from day one through to the exam.
