Admissions

Joining is straightforward.

  1. 01

    Enquire

    Call, message, or send the form below. Tell us the learner's grade and the subjects they need.

  2. 02

    Meet & assess

    We meet the learner, talk through goals, and run a short baseline so we place them correctly.

  3. 03

    Enrol & register

    Complete enrolment and, where needed, we register you for NSSCO or NAMCOL examinations as a centre.

  4. 04

    Start ringing results

    Classes begin, mentorship kicks in, and progress is tracked from day one through to the exam.
