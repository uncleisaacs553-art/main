Good questions

Things people ask.

How much does a website cost?

Starter sites are a one-off N$1,500 for three mobile-friendly pages. Need something smaller or bigger? There's a budget One-Page option and a larger Business package too — and you only pay a 50% deposit to begin.

I already have a Facebook page — why a website?

Facebook is great for posts, but you don't own it and customers struggle to find your hours, location and prices. A website pulls everything together, shows up on Google, and works alongside your Facebook — they're best together.

How long does it take?

Most Starter sites go live in about 1–2 weeks, once we have your deposit and your content (logo, photos, business details). Got a deadline? Tell us and we'll work to it.

How do I pay?

Simple: FNB eWallet, MTC Mobile Money, or cash — no bank card needed on your side. 50% to start, 50% when your site goes live.

What about hosting and a domain name?

You can start free on a shared web address. When you're ready, a custom .com.na domain is about N$1,500/year (billed separately) — we set it all up for you.

Can you make it cheaper?

Yes — we can start with a single-page site to fit your budget, then add pages later as your business grows.
